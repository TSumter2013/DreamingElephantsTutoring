Precision tips — the 1300 to 1500 layer
SAT Math
  • Vertex form h(x) = a(x−h)²+k reveals max/min instantly — memorize it
  • Desmos: graph both sides and find intersection — often faster than solving
  • Systems: elimination over substitution for speed
  • Word problems: underline what is being solved before touching a number
  • Open-ended (no answer choices): check if your answer is reasonable before entering
  • Module 1 = gatekeeper. Slow is smooth. Smooth is fast.
SAT Reading
  • Read the question FIRST — know what you are hunting before the passage
  • Right answer is always provable from the text — point to the line or eliminate it
  • Extreme language (always, never, only) = eliminate that answer first
  • Never change an answer unless you found specific proof it is wrong
  • Cross-text questions: identify agreement vs disagreement before reading the options
  • Standard English: read the sentence aloud in your head — wrong answers sound wrong
ACT Math & English
  • No Desmos on paper ACT — know quadratic formula cold before June 13
  • 4 answer choices not 5 — process of elimination is more powerful here
  • ACT Math covers more Geometry and Trig than SAT — the drilling carries over
  • ACT English: comma rules, subject-verb agreement, pronoun clarity = free points
  • Transitions questions: identify logical relationship first (contrast, addition, cause-effect)
  • Do not leave any question blank on either test — no penalty for guessing
ACT Science
  • This is NOT a science test — it is data reading with science vocabulary
  • Read questions FIRST, then go to the graph or passage for the answer
  • Conflicting Viewpoints passage: identify each scientist's claim before answering anything
  • Track the axes on every graph carefully — units and scale matter
  • Engineering brain advantage: you already interpret graphs and experimental data naturally
  • Do not spend time memorizing science facts — the answers are always in the passage
Test-day execution
  • First 5 questions of every module: slow down, get them all right
  • Skip + flag: more than 90 seconds on a question means flag it and move on
  • Never leave a question blank on SAT or ACT — guess if you have to
  • If two answers both seem right: re-read the question exactly as written
  • Pacing: if consistently finishing modules early, going too fast
  • Night before: no cramming, early sleep, protein breakfast, know the test center

A 2025 partnership with Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University allows ECSU students to earn a B.S. in Aviation Science and an Embry-Riddle M.S. in Aviation in just 5 years. ECSU is a United Airlines Aviate partner. New flight training facilities at Currituck Airport open Fall 2026. Concentrations include Flight Education, Aviation Management, Professional Aeronautics/ATC, and Unmanned Aircraft Systems. Long-standing partnerships with the FAA and NASA.
🌟 ECSU aviation graduates flying for major and regional airlines, Coast Guard, and corporate aviation · alumni in FAA and defense aviation roles
Texas Southern University
Houston, TX · Public HBCU · Urban Houston
Aviation Science Management B.S. (Part 141) United Airlines Aviate partner American Airlines partnership NASA Johnson Space Center 30 min away Civil Engineering + Electrical & Computer Engineering B.S.
Texas Southern operates a FAA Part 141 flight school at Houston Hobby Airport (KHOU), 20 minutes from campus. Aviation concentrations include Professional Pilot, Aviation Management, and Drone/UAS. TSU is one of the United Airlines Aviate HBCU partners and has an American Airlines partnership for networking and conditional job offers. Civil Engineering and Electrical & Computer Engineering B.S. degrees are also offered. Houston’s proximity to NASA Johnson Space Center — about 30 minutes from campus — creates a natural aerospace career pipeline. TSU’s location in Houston also puts students near one of the most diverse and active aerospace and energy industries in the world.
🌟 TSU aviation graduates in airline, military, and corporate aviation roles · engineering graduates throughout Texas’s aerospace and energy sectors
